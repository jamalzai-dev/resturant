# Pizza Max demo photography

These stock photographs are retained as unused alternatives. The current menu uses the 53 generated illustrations documented in [docs/menu-image-provenance.json](docs/menu-image-provenance.json). Neither set shows a verified Pizza Max business or establishes the exact appearance of a real restaurant's dishes.

All six source pages below identify their photographs as free Pexels stock. The [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/) permits free use and modification, including websites and templates. Photographer attribution is included here even though it is not required. Do not imply photographer or pictured-brand endorsement, resell these images as unaltered prints, or redistribute them as a stock-photo collection. Sources and license checked on 23 October 2026.

Each image is stored as 640px and 1440px-wide WebP files, retaining the original aspect ratio. `node scripts/download-demo-assets.mjs` re-downloads and optimizes these assets using Sharp. Files remain local at runtime; there are no requests to photo services when visitors browse the website.

The pre-existing stock images and their sources are documented separately in `PHOTO_CREDITS.md`. Replace image paths and bilingual alt text in `src/config/siteImages.ts` when customizing this template for a real restaurant.
