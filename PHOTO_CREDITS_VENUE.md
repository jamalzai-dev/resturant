# Venue photography credits

These seven stock photographs show different venues and people. They illustrate the demo atmosphere; they are not Pizza Max premises, employees, guests, or endorsements. No food-only imagery is used in the gallery.

All selected images are available under the [Pexels license](https://www.pexels.com/license/), checked 26 October 2026. It permits use in websites and templates and image modification; do not redistribute the files as a standalone stock library, imply endorsement, or use identifiable people offensively. Keep the visible demo/stock labels until replacing them with authorized client photography.

Only responsive 640px and 1440px WebP derivatives are served from `public/images/venue/`. Cropping is controlled by CSS; no generative alterations were made. Photographs were chosen for warm lighting, wood finishes and an informal dining atmosphere; they are not a single-venue photo shoot.
